# Nature Park Design

When designing a nature/park scene, follow these conventions:

- **Dense Forests:** Create thick clusters of mixed tree types (green and yellow). Vary density — some areas should be dense forest, others sparse woodland.
- **Clearings:** Leave open grassy areas within forests as natural clearings. These make good spots for mushroom patches.
- **Paths:** Use winding dirt paths that curve through the forest. Avoid straight grid-like paths — nature is organic.
- **Water Features:** If the tileset supports it, place ponds or streams. Otherwise, use clearings to suggest open space.
- **Mushrooms:** Scatter mushroom clusters in shaded areas near large trees. They thrive in forest edges and clearings.
- **Bushes:** Use bushes to create natural borders between different zones (forest edge, path borders, clearing boundaries).
- **No Buildings:** Parks should feel wild and natural. Avoid houses and castles unless the user specifically requests a ranger station or similar.
- **Decor:** Place signs at path intersections and beehives near flower patches.

The overall feel should be a tranquil, wild natural space — like a national park trail with varied terrain and points of interest.
