# Medieval Village Design

When designing a village scene, follow these conventions:

- **Layout:** Place a central town square with paths radiating outward. Houses should cluster around the square.
- **Houses:** Use warm-colored roofs (brown/red) for commoner houses. Vary house sizes between 3×3 and 5×5.
- **Castle:** If the area is large enough (15×15+), place a castle at the top-center as the focal point.
- **Fences:** Surround the village perimeter with fences. Use partial fences with gates on main path entries.
- **Nature:** Fill empty areas between houses with trees and bushes. Scatter mushrooms sparingly near forest edges.
- **Paths:** Connect all buildings with dirt paths. Create a main road from the gate to the castle/town center.
- **Decor:** Place signs near the village entrance, crates near houses, and wheelbarrows near fields.

The overall feel should be a cozy, functional medieval settlement with clear zoning: nature on the outskirts, residential in the middle, and civic/military (castle) at the center or top.
